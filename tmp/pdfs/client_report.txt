WALIMA — App Onboarding Flow

Developer Reference | May 2026



   Onboarding


Welcome Screen (Login)
   Full-screen couple photo background with Walima logo

   4 login buttons: Continue with Phone (primary pink), Continue with Email (glass),
   Apple + Google (side by side)

   Help modal: “Trouble with OTP?” + “Contact support”

   Language selector: English




OTP / Email Verify
   Phone OTP: WhatsApp first → SMS fallback, 6-digit input, 30s timer, auto-focus + paste
   support

   Email OTP: Same 6-digit layout, masked email, 30s timer

   Verify & Log In button




Step 1 — Profile For + Gender
   Profile For (single select chips): Myself, Son, Daughter, Brother, Sister, Friend, Relative

   Gender (single select chips): Male, Female




Step 2 — Name + DOB
   First Name — text input
   Last Name — text input

   Date of Birth — custom dark modal picker (Day / Month / Year scrollers), auto age
   calculation, 18+ validation




Step 3 — Religion + Country + City
   Religion (dropdown): Open To All, Islam, Hinduism, Christianity, Buddhism, Others

   Country (dropdown, searchable): Bangladesh default + 20+ countries

   City (dropdown, searchable, dynamic): Changes based on country. Bangladesh = 64
   districts




Step 3B — Religious Practice (NEW, after Religion)
   Title: “How important is faith in your daily life?”

   5 options (radio cards):

       Very religious — “I pray 5 times, fast regularly, and follow my faith strictly every day”

       Moderately religious — “I try to pray and follow my faith but sometimes I miss”

       Somewhat religious — “I practise mainly during Ramadan, Eid, and other special
       occasions”

       Not very religious — “I believe in my faith but don’t actively practise”

       Prefer not to say — “I’d rather keep this private”

   Skip option. Auto-skip if “Open To All” religion selected




Step 3C — Marriage Intention (NEW, after Religious Practice)
   Title: “What are your plans for marriage?”

   Q1: “I’d like to know someone on Walima for” — 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months,
   1-2 years

   Q2: “I’d like to get married within” — Within 6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-3
   years, No rush, Decide together

   Both required to continue. Skip option available
Step 4 — Email + Phone
   Email Address — text input, email validation

   Phone Number — country code selector (42 countries with flags, BD +880 default) +
   phone input




Step 5 — Family (Yes/No) + Residency
   You Live with Your Family — Yes / No toggle

   Residency Status (dropdown): Citizen, Permanent Resident, Work Visa, Student Visa,
   Temporary Visa, Other




Step 6 — Marital Status + Height + Diet
   Marital Status (dropdown): Never Married, Married, Divorced, Widowed, Separated

   Height (dropdown, scrollable): 4ft 6in to 7ft 0in (shows ft/in + cm)

   Dietary Preference (chips, optional): Halal Only, Non Vegetarian, Vegetarian, Vegan,
   Occasionally Non-Veg




Step 7 — Education + Institute
   Highest Qualification (dropdown): Below SSC, SSC/O-Level, HSC/A-Level, Diploma,
   Bachelor’s/Honours, Master’s, PhD/Doctorate, Professional Degree, Hafez/Islamic
   Studies, Other

   Institute / University — text input

   Additional Institute — text input (optional)




Step 8 — Income + Work Sector + Profession + Company
   Annual Income (optional): Yearly/Monthly toggle, income range dropdown (dynamic
   BDT ranges), hide income toggle

   Work Sector (dropdown): Private Company, Government, Self-employed, Business
   Owner, Freelance, NGO, Not Working, Student, Retired, Other
   Profession (dropdown, searchable): 18 options — Banker/Finance, IT/Software, Doctor,
   Engineer, Lawyer, Accountant, Businessman, Teacher, Architect, Journalist, Government
   Officer, Military, Pharmacist, Nurse, Freelancer, Student, Homemaker, Other

   Company / Organisation — text input (optional)




Step 9 — About Yourself (bio)
   Bio textarea — max 500 chars, min 20 chars, character counter

   Quick add chips: “Family oriented.” “Practicing Muslim.” “Love to travel.” “Career
   focused.” “Simple and humble.” “Looking for a life partner who shares my values.”

   Helper: “A well-written bio helps you get 3x more profile views”




Photo Upload
   Upload area: dashed circle + “Upload Image” + “Use Camera”

   Photo preview with X remove

   Photo Guidelines accordion (Good: Close Up, Half View, Full View / Bad: Side Face,
   Group, Unclear)

   Know More accordion (formats, size limits, rules)

   Skip: “Add Photos Later >”




Hobbies & Interests
   Minimum 5 selections required

   10 categories (86+ items total):

   1. Creative (15) — Writing, Cooking, Singing, Photography, Painting, etc.

   2. Reading & Learning (8) — Books, Podcasts, Online courses, etc.

   3. Outdoor Activities (10) — Hiking, Camping, Swimming, etc.

   4. Fitness & Sports (15) — Running, Yoga, Cricket, Football, etc.

   5. Travel & Leisure (9) — Solo travel, Road trips, Adventure, etc.

   6. Food & Lifestyle (7) — Cooking, Baking, Street food, etc.
   7. Faith & Community (7) — Quran recitation, Volunteering, Charity, etc.

   8. Technology & Gaming (7) — Coding, Gaming, AI & Tech, etc.

   9. Entertainment (8) — Movies, TV series, Board games, etc.

  10. Social & Lifestyle (8) — Socializing, Shopping, Fashion, etc.

   Skip option available




Family Details (optional)
   Father’s Status (dropdown): Employed, Self-employed/Business, Retired, Not
   Employed, Deceased, Prefer not to say

   Mother’s Status (dropdown): Homemaker, Employed, Self-employed/Business, Retired,
   Deceased, Prefer not to say

   No. of Brothers + Sisters (side by side dropdowns): None to 10+

   Family Type (dropdown): Joint Family, Nuclear Family, Other

   All optional. Skip button available




Financial Status (optional)
   4 options (radio cards):

      Wealthy — “Affluent family with significant assets”

      Upper Middle Class — “Comfortable lifestyle with good income”

      Middle Class — “Stable family with moderate income”

      Prefer not to say — “Keep this information private”

   Skip button available

   “Well done!” message

   Mother Tongue (dropdown): Open To All, Bangla, English, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Malay,
